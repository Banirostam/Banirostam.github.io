## Editorial Activities

### Guest Editor — AppliedMath (MDPI)

**Special Issue:** Mathematical Models and Optimization for Intelligent Decision Support Systems

I serve as Guest Editor for this Special Issue, which focuses on mathematical modeling, optimization, and computational approaches to decision-making under uncertainty and practical constraints.

The Special Issue welcomes contributions involving data-driven optimization, intelligent decision support, robust and multi-objective decision models, and applications in areas such as healthcare, engineering, finance, and operations.
